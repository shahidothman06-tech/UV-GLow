Last updated: 25 October 2026

**What the app does:** UV Glow shows a violet light effect on your screen and can switch on your phone's normal white flashlight LED. It does not produce real ultraviolet (UV) light.

**Information stored on your device:** your settings, themes and presets are saved only on your phone and may be included in your Android device backup. We do not receive this information.

**Advertising:** the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information and ad interactions to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings › Privacy & ad choices. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings. How Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Camera and flashlight:** the app only turns the flashlight LED on and off. It never takes photos or video.

**Purchases:** a future version may offer Premium through Google Play. Payments are handled by Google Play, and we never see your payment details.

**Children:** the app is not directed at children under 13.

**Data sharing:** we do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising data collected by Google AdMob, the app does not collect or share personal data.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, we will update this page and the date.
